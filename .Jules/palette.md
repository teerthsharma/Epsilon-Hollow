## 2025-02-09 - Accessible Console Panel
**Learning:** Dynamic telemetry/thought streams need explicit `role="log"` and `aria-live="polite"` to ensure screen readers correctly announce new content without interrupting the user. Icon-only buttons must have `title`, `aria-label`, and `focus-visible` styles.
**Action:** Always add `role="log"` and `aria-live="polite"` to log containers. Always add full accessibility to icon-only buttons.
